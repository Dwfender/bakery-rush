Bakery Rush

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Bakery Rush does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The game has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your scores, streak and settings are saved only on your device and are never sent anywhere.
  • The game does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • When you tap “Share result”, iOS opens its share sheet and you choose where the text goes. The game does not see or keep it.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
